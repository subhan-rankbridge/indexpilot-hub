IndexPilot — Own-Engine Article Indexing

IndexPilot is an article indexing checker and submission service with its own engine: submitted links are listed publicly so search crawlers can reach them. No third-party indexing APIs.

Public hub of submitted links: indexpilot-tawny.vercel.app/hub
